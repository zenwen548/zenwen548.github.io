How I work

Three habits that show up in every project above.

Honest models over pretty numbers

The churn project's headline is a 0.758 AUC because the perfect-looking score was leakage, and the writeups say so. Limitations are documented, not hidden.

Reproducible by strangers

Pinned dependencies, sample data that ships, Docker entrypoints, MLflow tracking. If it cannot be rerun from a fresh clone, it is not done.

Built for the decision

Every model ends in something a stakeholder can use: risk tiers, revenue at risk, and drilldowns from the aggregate to the exact customers to call.
